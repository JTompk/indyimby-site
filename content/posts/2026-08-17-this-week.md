This week's dockets carry 13 items with no new filings since last Monday, and the significant action is all at Wednesday's Metropolitan Development Commission hearing (Aug 19). On the multifamily side, 2026-ZON-060 seeks to rezone the 1.54-acre site at 1305 Broad Ripple Avenue from D-5 to D-P for a mixed-use project with 94 multifamily dwellings, and 2026-REG-015 proposes a six-story building at 412 West McCarty Street downtown across from Lucas Oil. On the incentive side, the MDC will take up four tax-abatement items: paired petitions 2026-A-042 and 2026-A-043 at 6400 Kentucky Avenue, and paired petitions 2026-A-016 and 2026-A-017 at 2505 North Sherman Drive for the MetroBloks data center. The remaining seven items are routine variances and special exceptions at Tuesday's BZA III hearing — nothing multifamily among them.
